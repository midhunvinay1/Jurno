# Code of Conduct

LIFE is a welcoming project for people with different bodies, cultures, identities, access needs, and relationships with productivity.

Be respectful, assume good faith, and critique ideas rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized attention, threats, or sharing another person's private life data are not welcome.

Maintainers may remove comments, contributions, or access that violate these expectations. Report a concern privately through the repository's security reporting channel when it is available. Please do not publish sensitive reports or personal information in public issues.
